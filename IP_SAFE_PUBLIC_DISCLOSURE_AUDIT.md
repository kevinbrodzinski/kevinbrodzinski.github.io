# KevinBrodzinski.com — IP-Safe Public Disclosure Audit

Status: conservative public-disclosure pass completed across Home, Research, and Patents.

## Redaction doctrine

Public pages retain: company/product names, high-level problem statements, non-enabling architectural roles, already-public research titles/summaries, high-level invention genealogy, and public benchmark positioning.

Public pages withhold or generalize: unpublished provisional titles, application numbers, exact filing chronology beyond broad year-level story, claim language, implementation-enabling mechanism lists, source code, model/operator parameters, detailed cryptographic constructions, security rules, routing/settlement algorithms, continuation/divisional strategy, and patent-only embodiments.

## Page assessment

- Home: reduced detailed internal mechanics for Governed Creation, TraceScript, Action Admissibility, Sphira, Muon, and Sensitivity to public-safe role descriptions. Added an explicit public IP boundary.
- Research: retained public publication material; added notice that unpublished implementations, patent-only embodiments, parameterizations, and claim strategy are excluded.
- Patents: removed the highest-risk disclosure layer: exact provisional titles in the chapter archive, mechanism tags, precise filing-day chronology, detailed process chains, and implementation-specific invention lists. Replaced them with family-level categories and confidentiality boundary language.

## Counsel review triggers

Before launch, obtain patent-counsel review if any of these apply: foreign/PCT protection is still being preserved; a page mentions subject matter not fully supported in a filed application; the subject matter is intended to remain a trade secret; a conversion/continuation filing is imminent; or a public research paper and pending patent overlap in ways not yet mapped.

## Public-source hardening

The audit also reviewed HTML/JavaScript source, not only rendered copy. Detailed canvas labels, hidden architecture descriptions, execution-stage names, exact mechanism tags, and the patent-to-product coverage matrix were generalized or removed so that material is not exposed simply by viewing page source.
