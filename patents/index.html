PUBLIC STATUS DISCLOSURE

This public page intentionally presents only high-level invention genealogy and product-relevant categories. Unpublished provisional specifications, application numbers, exact filing chronology, claims, enabling embodiments, source code, parameters, security constructions, continuation strategy, and other non-public know-how are withheld. A provisional application is not an issued patent, and the visual family maps are not legal claim charts or patentability opinions. Detailed technical diligence should occur under an appropriate confidentiality process.
